## Skill-up

**1.5h every day** (given 2026-07-27 — supersedes the earlier 1h
"skilling hour" standing assumption). Placement within the day's work
windows still needs to be asked/confirmed each day, same as before.

Ad-hoc calls (e.g. a one-off status call with a specific person, or a
one-off production-deployment call mentioned in a day's brain dump) are
not part of this standing schedule and should be captured in that day's
brain dump instead.
